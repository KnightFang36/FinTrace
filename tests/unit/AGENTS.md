# tests/unit instructions

## Purpose
Verify individual implemented rules, graph operations and workflow policies.

## Inputs
Minimal deterministic records and explicit policies.

## Outputs
Assertions on evidence, boundary conditions and errors.

## Functionality
Cover valid patterns and legitimate counterexamples, equal timestamps, currency separation, insufficient history, score-family caps and legal lifecycle transitions.

## Constraints
Do not assert only internal implementation details. No tests are supplied for unimplemented services.
